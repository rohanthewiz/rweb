**N-063 (`serveConn`, `Server.go`):**
- **The bug:** with no version field, the code assumed a two-byte CRLF ending and cut the target as `message[space+1 : len-2]`.
  - After a bare LF, `GET /xy\n` lost its last byte and was routed to `/x`.
  - `GET \n` sliced `[4:3]` and panicked. The panic was recovered as a 500, with a logged stack trace.
- **The fix:**
  - The line ending is trimmed once, with `TrimRight "\r\n"`, which the version field already used.
  - The target and version are cut from the trimmed line. A small diagram of the three line shapes is in the comment there.
  - An empty target (`GET `, `GET  HTTP/1.1`) is answered with 400 and the connection is closed.
- **Tests:** `request_line_test.go`, run over tcp and tls. It covers CRLF and bare LF, with and without a version, and HTTP/1.0.

**N-069 (`Clean`, `Context.go`):**
- **The cap:** `maxRetainedHeaders` = 256 entries (8 KB). A list grown past it goes back to `newContext`'s capacity of 8. This is the same pattern N-041 uses for the body buffers.
- **Zeroing:** `clear()` runs on both lists before they are truncated.
  - Without it, slots past `len` stay visible to the GC. A kept array would then hold on to the previous request's strings, such as a 1 MB header value.
  - The cost is a memclr of len×32 bytes per request.
- **Safety:** the clear is only safe because `Clean` runs on wire contexts only. The synthetic `s.Request` stores the caller's own `headers` slice and never calls `Clean`. I checked both `Clean` call sites (`Server.go`).
- **Tests:** `TestCleanReleasesOversizedHeaderLists` and `TestCleanKeepsSmallHeaderListsZeroed` in `buffer_retention_test.go`. I confirmed both fail with the cap and the clear edited out of `Clean`.

**N-067 (`websocket.go`):**
- `SetMaxMessageSize` stores into the atomic. `readFrame` and `ReadMessage` load from it, at the size check and at both decompress calls.
- **Test:** `TestWebSocketSetMaxMessageSizeDuringReads` in `websocket_concurrency_test.go`. Before the change, `-race` flagged `websocket.go:1347` (the setter) against `:856` (`readFrame`).

**N-071, part (`d746e23`):**

  - That is well past the "few lines" the user approved, so I stopped there and recorded the full scope and two options on N-071.

- **For the rest:** I checked `runUntilSignal` and confirmed that a direct `Shutdown` makes `Run` return cleanly. So the remaining tests could keep `Run` and call `s.Shutdown` in place of the signal.
